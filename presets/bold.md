# Preset: Bold colorful

## Theme
A gig poster on a sunny wall: big flat color fields, oversized type and playful confidence. Loud on purpose, but every block has one job.

## Color
- bg #fff8ec (cream), fg #1a1423 (near-black), muted #4f4659, surface #ffe3b8 (apricot), line #1a1423.
- accent #1f5eff (cobalt) for the main action and one color block per screen.
- Color comes from full-width flat section backgrounds (bg-surface, bg-accent), never gradients.

## Typography
- Display: a heavy, characterful sans (e.g. Archivo Black, Bricolage Grotesque). Body: a friendly sans (e.g. DM Sans).
- Headings huge: text-6xl/md:text-8xl, leading-none, uppercase allowed for short lines.
- Body 18px, short paragraphs, plain language.

## Components
- Thick 2px borders in line color around images and buttons; square or slightly rounded corners.
- Sticker-like labels rotated a few degrees, used once or twice per page.
- Big numbered steps; oversized quote marks for testimonials.
- Buttons: solid accent with cream text, chunky padding, border-2 border-line.

## Layout
- Full-bleed color bands alternating with cream sections.
- Asymmetric splits (7/5) and one section that breaks the grid with an oversized word.
- Vertical rhythm: py-20 to py-28.

## Motion
- Bold: TextReveal hero, Marquee band of short phrases, Magnetic main button, Tilt on a few cards.

## Do / Don't
- Do pick one surprising move per section and commit to it.
- Don't mix more than two background colors in one section or add drop shadows.
